Why FinGraph
exists.

Financial data is deeply connected — but the tools analysts use are not.

Fragmented sources

Filings, metrics, news, and relationships live in separate silos. Connecting them takes hours of manual work.

Opaque answers

LLM answers sound confident but hide their sources. You can't verify what you can't trace.

Static screens

Dashboards show snapshots. They don't reveal how a metric flows from a segment to a filing to a company.

FinGraph was built to replace that friction with a single intelligence layer: a knowledge graph that connects every metric, relationship, filing, and event — then surfaces exactly what the evidence supports, with the full provenance path visible.

How it works
in three steps.

From question to verified insight — no black boxes.

  1. 01

    Ask

    Type any financial question — "What was Apple's Services revenue in FY25?" or "Compare NVDA vs AMD gross margins." The router classifies intent and identifies the relevant issuer(s).

  2. 02

    Retrieve

    The graph engine traverses the knowledge graph: Company → Filings → Metrics → Segments → Evidence chunks. Only real relationships from SEC filings are used — no hallucinated connections.

  3. 03

    Verify

    The grader checks every sentence against the retrieved evidence. Each claim gets a verdict: Supported, Qualified, or Refused. The full provenance trail — tags, rules, figures — is always visible.
